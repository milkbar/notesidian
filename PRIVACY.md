# Privacy Policy

**This page has moved to the Notesidian website:**

## 👉 <https://milkbar.github.io/notesidian/privacy.html>

Short version: Notesidian collects **no personal data**. No tracking, no analytics, no servers.
It reads and writes only the Obsidian vault folder you choose, makes no network connections of
its own, and everything stays on your Mac.

Questions about privacy? [Open an issue](https://github.com/milkbar/notesidian/issues) on GitHub.
